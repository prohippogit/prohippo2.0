About ProHippo

ProHippo is practice-management software built for chartered accountants, tax practitioners and their teams. It brings a professional’s entire workload — clients, matters, hearings, notices, orders, appeals, invoices and client communication — into one calm, organised workspace.

What ProHippo does

  • Keeps every assessee’s proceedings, notices and orders in one place;
  • Reads notices and orders with AI to capture sections, assessment years, deadlines and demands;
  • Tracks appeal and hearing deadlines so nothing slips;
  • Helps prepare appeals before the CIT(A) and ITAT;
  • Lets professionals communicate with clients — including via WhatsApp, with the client’s consent.
